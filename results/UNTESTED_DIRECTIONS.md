**Cost tags.**
- `[cloud]` can run here with data we already have.
- `[GPU]` needs the person's PC (RTX 5060).
- `[data]` needs outside data that the cloud can't reach. That usually means the PC fetching it into `regional/incoming/`, or a source we don't have.
- `[person]` needs the person to decide or act.

**Status tags.** **OPEN** = not started. **RUNNING** = in progress now. **PARTIAL** = some of it done, with the gap named. **DONE** = finished, with its folder, so nobody repeats it. **DECLINED** = the person said no.
